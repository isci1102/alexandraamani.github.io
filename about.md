## My background

I’m Alexandra. I grew up in Côte d’Ivoire and now live in France, where I’m pursuing a PhD in economics. My background brings together economics, statistics, and data science.

## What interests me

I’m drawn to questions about information and institutions, and to the ways data can help us understand them. I enjoy work that brings a meaningful question together with a thoughtful empirical approach.

This website is a home for that work, but also for projects and ideas that sit outside the boundaries of my PhD.

## Community

Outside my research, I volunteer at a homeless shelter. Community involvement is a part of my life that I want to make space for here, too.

## A few other things

I speak **French and English**. I’m building this space in English so that more people can explore my work.
